Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build my website?

It depends on the size of the project (number of pages, features). After a first conversation, we give you a precise timeline in the quote.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on your needs: showcase site, institutional site, sales page or SaaS platform. Message us on WhatsApp for a free, tailored quote.

Do you handle the domain name and going live?

Yes. We put your website online and connect it to your own domain name (for example www.yourcompany.com).

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. All our websites are designed to look great on phones, tablets and computers — most of your customers will find you on mobile.

Do you work with clients outside Niger?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses, coaches and institutions across Africa and around the world.

Can my website be updated after delivery?

Yes. Text, photos, new pages: updates are handled with us, and the terms are set out in the quote.
